Privacy Policy

ThreadAndGrin · Last updated September 10, 2026

ThreadAndGrin ("we," "us") is a small print-on-demand apparel shop. This policy explains what information we handle and how. We keep it simple because our operation is simple.

Where our shop lives

We sell through our Etsy storefront at etsy.com/shop/ThreadAndGrin. When you place an order, your purchase, payment, and account information are collected and processed by Etsy under Etsy's Privacy Policy. We only receive the order details Etsy shares with us to fulfill your purchase (such as your name and shipping address).

How orders are fulfilled

Our products are printed and shipped on demand by our fulfillment partner, Printful. To ship your order, the shipping details you provide at checkout are shared with Printful solely to produce and deliver your item. See Printful's Privacy Policy for details.

Information we collect

We do not run our own website store, and we do not directly collect, store, or sell personal data outside of the order information Etsy provides to fulfill purchases. We do not build marketing lists from your data.

Social media and marketing

We share images of our products on social platforms, including Pinterest, to help people discover our shop. These posts link back to our public Etsy listings. We do not access or collect personal information about you through these platforms; any data you share with those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies.

Cookies and tracking

Because we operate on Etsy rather than our own site, any cookies or analytics you encounter while shopping are managed by Etsy, not by us.

Your choices

You can request information about, correction of, or deletion of the order data we hold by contacting us. Account-level requests (such as closing your account or broader data requests) are handled through Etsy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Message us any time through our Etsy shop: etsy.com/shop/ThreadAndGrin.

We may update this policy as our shop grows. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
